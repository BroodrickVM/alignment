# Tool 2 demonstration guide

## Five-minute walkthrough

1. Open the dashboard and select SYNTHETIC_Atlas. State: all teams, scores and
   discussion notes in this demonstration are fictional.
2. Explain the summary: GrandMean 3.70; Teamwork is relatively stronger; Basic
   Needs is the lower hierarchy area. These are within-team patterns, not
   benchmark judgments or causes.
3. Open Employee Voice (Q07, 2.90). Explain that a direct conceptual link is
   different from direct research validation. The preliminary evidence outcome
   is partial support. No mapping is a validated communication diagnosis.
4. Open Evidence validation status. Nine initial reviews are recorded. Human
   review remains outstanding for all nine; three full-text access checks are
   pending. Initial review completion is not scientific validation.
5. In Manager Discussion Workflow, choose Q07 and enter the fictional example
   below. Save notes in the session, then download the Discussion Summary.
   Show the separation of observed score, hypothesis, employee observations,
   manager reflection and chosen next step.
6. Raise Minimum responses per item from 5 to 21. Atlas has 20 responses per
   item: the whole report and workflow disappear. Saved notes for newly
   suppressed groups are removed. Download the demo notes before this step.
   Restore 5; removed notes do not return.

## Fictional notes

Employee observations: Some team members said ideas are invited but updates on
suggestions are inconsistent; others described ideas that led to changes.

Assessment: Mixed or unclear.

Manager reflection: Experiences differ. Workload and meeting structure may also
contribute; the discussion does not establish what caused the survey score.

Decision: Agree on an action.

Next step: Trial a suggestion tracker for four weeks, including status and brief
reasons for decisions. Offer a private way to contribute.

Responsible role: Team lead. Follow-up: After the four-week trial.

## Presenter boundaries

Say “topic to explore” and “relative signal.” Avoid “communication caused this,”
“validated diagnostic,” or “guaranteed anonymity.” Notes are manager-entered and
unverified. The system does not use an LLM/RAG to infer causes. Synthetic scenario
checks verify software behavior, not scientific validity or real-world impact.
