### Sources and limits

Locke, E. A., & Latham, G. P. (2002). *Building a practically useful theory of goal
setting and task motivation: A 35-year odyssey.* American Psychologist, 57(9),
705–717. DOI: 10.1037/0003-066X.57.9.705.
[University-hosted paper](https://goal-lab.psych.umn.edu/orgPsych/2020/readings/5.%20Motivation/Locke%20%26%20Latham%20%282002%29.pdf).
Specificity, difficulty, task complexity and feedback matter together; clear goals
alone are not a diagnosis of communication effectiveness.

Bradler, C., Dur, R., Neckermann, S., & Non, A. (2014). *Employee Recognition and
Performance: A Field Experiment.* IZA Discussion Paper 8311.
[Working paper](https://docs.iza.org/dp8311.pdf).
A brief data-entry experiment with mainly student workers offers limited transfer
to ongoing teams. The tested intervention does not establish employees' preferred
recognition practices. This review used the 2014 version, not the final 2016 article.

Allen, T. D., Eby, L. T., Poteet, M. L., Lentz, E., & Lima, L. (2004). *Career
benefits associated with mentoring for protégés: A meta-analysis.* Journal of
Applied Psychology, 89(1), 127–136. DOI: 10.1037/0021-9010.89.1.127.
[Paper](https://www.same.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/career-benefits-associated-with-mentoring-for-proteges.pdf).
Mentoring encompasses career and psychosocial support; most contributing designs
do not resolve causal ordering. Objective career associations were small. This
supports questions about development, not a validated communication scale.

Kluger, A. N., & DeNisi, A. (1996). *The effects of feedback interventions on
performance: A historical review, a meta-analysis, and a preliminary feedback
intervention theory.* Psychological Bulletin, 119(2), 254–284.
DOI: 10.1037/0033-2909.119.2.254.
[Author-university abstract](https://cris.huji.ac.il/en/publications/the-effects-of-feedback-interventions-on-performance-a-historical/).
Feedback effects vary and can be negative. The intervention literature is not
equivalent to a survey item about having a progress conversation. Full-text
retrieval failed; appraisal beyond the abstract remains pending.
