## Q09 — Team Norms & Coordination

Havens, D. S., Vasey, J., Gittell, J. H., & Lin, W.-T. (2010).
*Relational coordination among nurses and other providers: impact on the quality
of patient care.* Journal of Nursing Management, 18(8), 926–937.
[Indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21073566/).
DOI: 10.1111/j.1365-2834.2010.01138.x.

Scope: indexed abstract only. Survey of 747 nurses; coordination and perceived
care quality were associated. Healthcare context and self-report measures limit
transfer. This supports asking about coordination, not identifying a coordination
failure from Q09. Full-text appraisal and a direct Q09 linkage remain pending.

## Q05 — Relational Support

Rhoades, L., & Eisenberger, R. (2002). *Perceived organizational support: a review
of the literature.* Journal of Applied Psychology, 87(4), 698–714.
[Indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12184574/).
DOI: 10.1037/0021-9010.87.4.698.

Scope: indexed abstract only. Review/meta-analysis covering more than 70 studies;
supervisor support was associated with perceived organizational support. This is
conceptual context for care, not validation of Q05 as a measure of respect,
responsiveness, or communication quality. Full-text appraisal remains pending.

## Q07 — Employee Voice

Morrison, E. W. (2014). *Employee Voice and Silence.* Annual Review of
Organizational Psychology and Organizational Behavior, 1, 173–197.
[Publisher abstract](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-031413-091328).
DOI: 10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-031413-091328.

Scope: publisher abstract only. Voice is voluntary upward input. This supports
exploring employees' input experiences but does not equate Q07 with actual
speaking-up behavior, psychological safety, or manager follow-through. Those
mechanisms and full-text appraisal remain pending. The original full claim is
only partially supported, even though the topic has a clear communication link.

## Q08 — Purpose Alignment

Grant, A. M. (2008). *The significance of task significance: Job performance
effects, relational mechanisms, and boundary conditions.* Journal of Applied
Psychology, 93(1), 108–124.
[Indexed abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18211139/).
DOI: 10.1037/0021-9010.93.1.108.

Scope: abstract and opening rationale inspected. Field experiments concern
beneficiary impact and task significance, not the mission-alignment mapping.
They offer contextual support for discussing meaningful work; their experimental
findings do not allow causal conclusions from Tool 2 aggregates. Full-text
appraisal and direct evidence on the mission-communication link remain pending.

## Q02 — Information & Resource Access

Deng, G., Huang, C., Cheung, S. P., & Zhu, S. (2021). *Job Demands and Resources
and Employee Well-Being in the Chinese Nonprofit Sector.* Frontiers in Psychology,
12, 780718. [Article](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.780718/full).
DOI: 10.3389/fpsyg.2021.780718.

Scope: conceptual framework, methods and limitations sections inspected. The
study uses information as a resource in a cross-sectional, self-report sample
of 233 Chinese foundation employees. It supports considering information access
as a discussion topic, not inferring it from Q02 equipment scores. Generalization,
causal inference, and equivalence to Q02 are unsupported.
